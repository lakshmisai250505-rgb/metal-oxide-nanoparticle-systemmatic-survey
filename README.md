# metal-oxide-nanoparticle-systemmatic-survey

## Overview

This repository contains the working framework and structured information for a systematic scientific survey of research articles on metal oxide nanoparticles.

The survey focuses on identifying, organizing, and comparing the characterization techniques used to study metal oxide nanoparticles across a collection of research articles.

## Scope of the Survey

The characterization techniques covered in the survey include:

- X-ray Diffraction (XRD)
- Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM)
- Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM)
- UV-Visible Spectroscopy
- Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR)
- Raman Spectroscopy
- Fluorescence/Photoluminescence
- X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy (XPS)
- Energy-Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDX/EDS)
- Brunauer-Emmett-Teller (BET) Surface Area Analysis
- Vibrating Sample Magnetometry (VSM)
- Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS)
- Thermogravimetric Analysis (TGA)
- Other relevant characterization techniques identified during the survey

## Objectives

The main objectives of this survey are to:

1. Identify the characterization techniques used across the selected research articles.
2. Extract reported experimental and characterization information from the original sources.
3. Organize the extracted information into structured datasets and comparison tables.
4. Compare reported properties such as crystallite size, particle size, morphology, crystal system, absorption wavelength, and band-gap energy where available.
5. Identify patterns and trends in the characterization of different metal oxide nanoparticles.
6. Develop a comprehensive characterization-technique checklist and statistical overview of the surveyed literature.

## Data Organization

The extracted information is being organized according to individual characterization techniques and article-wise records.

The survey includes structured datasets for:

- XRD characterization
- SEM/TEM morphology and particle-size information
- UV-Visible absorption wavelengths
- Band-gap values
- Spectroscopic characterization
- Surface and elemental characterization
- Thermal characterization
- Magnetic characterization
- Particle-size and surface-property characterization
- Overall characterization-technique statistics

## Data Verification

Information is being cross-checked against the original research articles before being incorporated into the structured datasets.

Particular attention is given to distinguishing between related but different parameters, such as:

- Crystallite size and particle size
- SEM/TEM particle size and DLS hydrodynamic size
- Absorption wavelength and band-gap energy
- Crystal system and crystal phase

Values that are not reported or cannot be reliably verified from the source are not inferred.

## References

The research articles considered in the survey will be documented using their bibliographic information and DOI where available.

Copyrighted full-text research articles are not redistributed through this repository.

## Status

This project is currently under development. The datasets and survey framework will be progressively updated as the article-wise information is verified and organized.

## Purpose

The repository is intended to support systematic organization, comparison, and analysis of scientific literature concerning the characterization of metal oxide nanoparticles.
